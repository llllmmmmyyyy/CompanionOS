# Privacy and child safety

## Implemented boundaries

Detailed meaningful WorldEvent records stay in Tablet WorldStore (bounded to 1000). Compact outcomes/status cross the local development relay and persist on Phone (up to 300 outcomes). The relay has ephemeral in-memory queues/results; restart does not replace the authoritative local journal. Preferences/files use the native app sandbox; this is not an encryption-at-rest claim or a production privacy certification. Clearing app data/uninstalling removes local data; no parent data-management UI or guaranteed remote deletion is claimed.

No camera monitoring, microphone monitoring, location tracking, emotion recognition or psychological diagnosis is implemented or requested. Interaction style, retries, foreground duration and explicit feedback are descriptive. No IQ, ADHD, ability, developmental or clinical attention assessment. Development emulator records are not a child study.

Movement completion is child-confirmed, not sensor/camera verified. Parent controls start/pause/resume/next/end and Child Mode exit. Sessions have finite foreground budgets; background pauses the Tablet, and the app does not claim reliable background reminders. Child Mode is in-app, not an OS lock. Adult supervision remains necessary. Activities are gentle indoor/seated options without sharp tools, climbing or jumping.

No loot boxes, random paid rewards, child-targeted purchases, FOMO or missed-day streak punishment. Daily all-mission completion can give a garden decoration once per date; skipped/incomplete adventures do not falsely count as completed. No automatic endless session start.

## Network and optional content

The Windows demo relay uses HTTP, loopback binding, room family-demo and guest gateway 10.0.2.2. It has no production authentication/TLS or isolated per-family authorization; do not expose it to a public/LAN service or use identifiable child information. No real child identities are required by the demonstration.

Optional Gemini/Huawei requests contain parent-supplied activity constraints/educational goals, not raw world event history. Provider data handling depends on that provider's actual terms; no cloud privacy certification is asserted. Credentials live only in backend environment variables. Generated outputs pass schema/safety checks and the default parent review gate before child playback; live authenticated generation is unverified. Curated offline content remains available on failure. See AI_ARCHITECTURE.md and AI_WORKFLOW.md.

My Video uses the real system picker and copies the selected MP4 into the Phone sandbox. If a parent explicitly publishes it for Large Screen playback, a copy goes to the local backend's ignored media directory. Do not import identifiable/private family video into a public demo. The relay/cache is development storage, not an authenticated private sharing product. Recorded judging video should exclude account screens, credentials, notifications with personal information and private media.

## Remaining review

Physical-device privacy/security review, accessible controls, adult/child usability, asset rights, age suitability, retention/deletion policy and any legal consent/compliance requirements are not certified by emulator or host tests. No claim of legal/clinical compliance is made.
